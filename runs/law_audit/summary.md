- double pendulum (double_pendulum). The energy formula may have a sign error in the coupling terms. In a separate check with 50 trajectories, the median relative energy drift is 0.357 for dt 0.01 and for dt 0.001.

- orbit near a black hole (Schwarzschild) (schwarzschild). The simulator stores E and L as constants, so the drift check tests nothing.
