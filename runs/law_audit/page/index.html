Simulator law audit

This page checks the 15 physics simulators in physwm, to help choose the laws for the next study.

It measures if one law makes all data, if conserved quantities stay constant, the force effect per step and the error of law-free predictors.

Six of 15 simulators use one law, all force effects are below the noise level of 0.1, and four simulators show a possible problem.

Summary

The force effect per step is the gap between the next state and a straight-line guess from the last two states. All values use the standard deviation of the training data as the unit. A law-free error is the best of five simple predictors that use no physics.
